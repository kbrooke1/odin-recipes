Tuna Fish Cakes

For more easy and delicious recipes, please visit our home.

Oven-baked tuna fish cakes with a soft, cheesy filling made from canned tuna, potato, corn and spring onion, wrapped in a crispy parmesan-panko crust and served with a sweet chilli lime sauce.

Ingredients

Tuna fish cakes

  • 2 medium russet potatoes
  • 425 g canned tuna in olive oil, drained and flaked
  • 75 g frozen corn
  • 90 g freshly grated colby or cheddar cheese
  • 30 g panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp whole-egg mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp freshly grated garlic
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt flakes
  • 1/4 tsp freshly craced black pepper
  • Olive oil spray

Parmesan panko crust

  • 60 g panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 25 g freshly grated parmesan

Sweet chilli and lime sauce

  • 150 g sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 tsp all-purpose soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp freshly grated ginger

Instructions

  1. Cook the potatoes - Place the whole potatoes in a microwave-safe dish with 60 ml (¼ cup) of water. Cover with a lid, leaving it slightly ajar, and microwave on High for 12 minutes, or until tender and easily pierced with a fork. Allow to cool slightly, then peel and mash until smooth. Set aside to cool slightly.
  2. Prepare the coating - Combine the panko breadcrumbs, paprika and parmesan in a shallow bowl. Set aside.
  3. Preheat the oven - Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F) (200°C/400°F fan-forced). Spray a large metal baking tray generously with olive oil spray. Do not line the tray
  4. Make the fish cake mixture - Add the mashed potato, tuna, corn, cheese, breadcrumbs, spring onion, egg, mustard, mayonnaise, garlic, salt and pepper to a large bowl. Mix until well combined. The mixture should be soft but hold its shape when pressed. If it feels too soft and sticky, add 1–2 tablespoons of extra panko breadcrumbs
  5. Shape and coat - Scoop approximately ¼ cup of the mixture at a time and shape into patties. Gently press each fish cake into the parmesan-panko coating, making sure all sides are lightly coated. Transfer to the prepared tray. Repeat to make approximately 12–14 fish cakes.
  6. Bake - Spray the tops generously with olive oil spray. Bake for 18–20 minutes, turning halfway through and spraying again with olive oil, until golden and crisp on the outside. Allow to rest for 5 minutes before serving.
  7. Make the sauce - Combine the sauce ingredients in a small bowl.
  8. Serve - Serve the tuna fish cakes warm with the sweet chilli and lime sauce, lime wedges and extra coriander.
